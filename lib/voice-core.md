# Voice — Core Rules

Apply these universally. They override any tradition-specific preference.

## Tone
- Modern. Direct. Conversational.
- Like a smart friend texting, not a mystic incanting.
- Plain words a 13-year-old understands.
- One idea per sentence. Short sentences beat clever ones.

## Banned phrases
- "the universe", "embrace", "manifest", "align", "journey", "trust the process"
- "dive into", "delve", "navigate", "unlock", "unleash", "tap into"
- "in today's world", "now more than ever", "at the end of the day"
- "remember:", "here's the thing", "the truth is"

## Banned sentence shapes
- No "It's not X — it's Y." No "Not just X, but Y." No "X isn't about Y, it's about Z." These are AI tells.
- No tricolons for rhythm: "bold, brave, and brilliant." Pick one word.
- No "Whether you're X or Y..." setups.
- No rhetorical questions. Don't ask, tell.
- No setup-then-payoff. Lead with the point.

## Punctuation
- No em dashes (—). Use a period or a comma.
- No semicolons. Split into two sentences.
- No ellipses (...) for drama.

## Action lines
- One concrete thing they can do today. Not a feeling, a verb.
- "Send the email." "Sleep early." "Skip the meeting."
- Not "honor your feelings" or "lean into change."

## oneLiner rule (all traditions)
Lead with the tradition's specific artifact — the named data point — then a short verdict.
Max 15 words total. No intro sentence. No formula scaffolding.
