# Tarot Card Interactions and Reading Techniques

## Suit Energies
- **Wands (Fire)**: Passion, inspiration, career, creative energy, action
- **Cups (Water)**: Emotions, relationships, intuition, the subconscious
- **Swords (Air)**: Mind, intellect, conflict, communication, truth
- **Pentacles (Earth)**: Material world, money, body, work, nature

## Elemental Dignity
Card pairs interact based on their elements. Always factor this into a multi-card spread.

**Friendly pairs** (mutually supporting — strengthen each other):
- Fire + Air (Wands + Swords): Each amplifies the other. Action meets decision. A Fire card next to an Air card intensifies both.
- Earth + Water (Pentacles + Cups): Grounding and nourishment. Practical matters supported by emotional depth.

**Inimical pairs** (opposing — weaken each other):
- Fire + Water (Wands + Cups): Fire and Water fight. Together they dilute both cards' meanings. Passion undermined by emotion, or vice versa. Neither reads at full strength.
- Earth + Air (Pentacles + Swords): Earth and Air are neutral-to-hostile. The practical vs. the mental. Neither supports the other.

**Neutral pairs**:
- Fire + Earth (Wands + Pentacles): Neither helps nor hurts. Read independently.
- Air + Water (Swords + Cups): Neutral. Mind and emotion coexist without amplifying or canceling.

**Same element** (doubled):
- Two cards of the same suit reinforce that suit's theme strongly. Two Wands = decisive action energy. Two Swords = a purely mental or conflict situation. Two Cups = deeply relational. Two Pentacles = material focus.

**Major Arcana** are elementally neutral — they transcend suit interactions but take on the energy of adjacent minor arcana cards.

## When Suits Dominate a Spread
- Mostly Wands: A time of action, ambition, career movement
- Mostly Cups: Emotional themes, relationship focus, inner work
- Mostly Swords: Mental stress, decisions, conflict, important communications
- Mostly Pentacles: Practical matters, financial concerns, physical health
- Mixed suits: Complex situation with multiple life areas in play

## Major vs. Minor Arcana
- **Many Major Arcana**: Significant life events, fate at work, deep soul lessons
- **Mostly Minor Arcana**: Everyday practical matters, more within personal control

## Position Logic (Three-Card Spread)
- **Past**: What led here — established energy, no longer the active force
- **Present**: The current active pressure — this is the loudest card
- **Future**: The trajectory if current energy continues unchecked

An inimical pair in Past + Present (e.g., Wands + Cups) suggests the past is actively undermining the present. A friendly pair in Present + Future (e.g., Swords + Wands) suggests momentum building.

## Key Card Combinations
- **The Tower + Death**: Profound transformation — the old structure must fully collapse
- **The Star after The Tower**: After crisis comes renewal and hope
- **The Lovers + Two of Cups**: Strong relationship energy, partnership theme
- **Ten of Pentacles + Four of Wands**: Lasting prosperity and domestic harmony
- **Three of Swords + The Moon**: Hidden grief, illusion causing emotional pain
- **Wheel of Fortune + The World**: A major cycle completing with positive outcome
- **The Hermit + Four of Cups**: Deep withdrawal and contemplation needed
- **Strength + King/Queen of any suit**: Mastery through inner work

## Reversed Cards
Reversed cards can indicate: blocked energy, internalized themes, delays, shadow expression, need to look within rather than without. Context determines which applies — a reversed Tower may mean the crisis is internal rather than external, not yet visible outside.
