# The 12 Houses

## House Classifications

**Angular houses (1, 4, 7, 10)** — The most powerful positions. Planets here act directly and prominently in the person's life. Angular planets demand to be noticed.

**Succedent houses (2, 5, 8, 11)** — Build on the angular houses. Planets here express more steadily and through accumulated effort.

**Cadent houses (3, 6, 9, 12)** — Associated with transition, learning, and hidden activity. Planets here are less direct but significant for mental development and inner life.

## The 12 Houses

1st House (Ascendant) — **Self, identity, appearance, physical body, first impressions.** The most personal point in the chart. Planets here color the entire personality.

2nd House — **Money, possessions, values, self-worth, personal resources.** What you own and what you value.

3rd House — **Communication, siblings, short travel, local environment, early education.** How you think and speak day-to-day.

4th House (IC) — **Home, family, roots, mother, private life, emotional foundation.** Where you come from; your private world.

5th House — **Creativity, romance, children, play, self-expression, pleasure.** Joy and what you create.

6th House — **Health, daily routine, work habits, service, wellness.** How you function day-to-day.

7th House (Descendant) — **Partnerships, marriage, open enemies, one-on-one relationships.** Mirrors the 1st — what you meet in others.

8th House — **Transformation, shared resources, death/rebirth, sexuality, inheritance, occult.** Deep change and merging.

9th House — **Philosophy, higher education, long travel, religion, law, beliefs.** Expansion of worldview.

10th House (MC/Midheaven) — **Career, public reputation, authority, life ambition.** How the world sees you professionally.

11th House — **Friends, groups, community, hopes and wishes, social causes.** Your tribe and your future vision.

12th House — **Hidden things, isolation, spirituality, karma, self-undoing, the unconscious.** What operates beneath awareness.

## Reading Planets in Houses
Always combine the planet (what), the sign (how), and the house (where/which life area). "Mars in Aries in the 7th" reads: aggressive/direct energy (Mars in Aries) playing out through partnerships and conflict with others (7th house).

Angular placements intensify. A planet in the 1st, 4th, 7th, or 10th will be louder than the same planet cadent.
