# Planetary Aspects

## The Five Major Aspects

**Conjunction (0°, orb 8°)** — Planets merge their energies. Intense focus. Can be harmonious or challenging depending on planets involved. The most powerful aspect.

**Sextile (60°, orb 6°)** — Easy flowing opportunity. Talents that require some action to activate. Softer than a trine.

**Square (90°, orb 8°)** — Tension and challenge driving action. Friction that motivates growth through conflict. The most action-forcing aspect.

**Trine (120°, orb 8°)** — Natural harmony and ease. Talents that flow effortlessly, though can lead to complacency without challenge.

**Opposition (180°, orb 8°)** — Polarity and tension seeking balance. Often plays out through relationships or external conflict.

## Applying vs. Separating
This distinction determines whether an aspect is gaining or losing power.

- **Applying**: The faster-moving planet is moving toward the exact angle. The aspect is building — this is the active pressure. **Applying aspects are more important to interpret than separating ones.**
- **Separating**: The faster planet has passed the exact angle. The aspect is fading — context, not the main driver.

Always ask: is this aspect tightening or loosening? A 2° applying Mars square is more urgent than a 7° separating one.

## Orb Strength
- **Tight (0–3°)**: Major, unavoidable — the chart's dominant theme right now.
- **Medium (3–6°)**: Active but not overwhelming.
- **Wide (6–8°)**: Background noise — mention only if no tighter aspects dominate.

## Key Aspect Combinations
- **Sun–Moon**: Ego and emotion in sync (conjunction/trine) or at war (square/opposition)
- **Sun–Saturn**: Discipline shapes identity — achievement or chronic restriction
- **Venus–Mars**: Desire and love in tension or harmony
- **Jupiter–Saturn**: Expansion vs. consolidation — the great rhythm of growth
- **Mercury–Neptune**: Intuitive communication or confusion and self-deception
- **Mars–Saturn**: Drive blocked by structure — frustration, endurance, or strategic patience
- **Jupiter–Uranus**: Sudden breakthroughs and unexpected expansion

## Retrograde Planets
A retrograde planet's energy turns inward. Mercury retrograde: communication and logic go internal, prone to misunderstanding. Saturn retrograde: internal authority, self-discipline questioned. Note retrogrades — they change the planet's expression significantly.
